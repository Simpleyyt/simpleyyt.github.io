Tare Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-11-20

What Tare does

Tare is an independent client for coding agents running on computers you choose. Tare does not provide a cloud account or model subscription. This policy describes the iPhone app.

Data on your device

Tare stores connection settings, device names, project and session information, recent conversation history, drafts, read progress, and preferences on your device. SSH private keys and SSH passwords are stored using iOS Keychain. Other connection information, including direct bridge credentials, is stored in app storage. Protect access to your device and the computers you connect to.

Data sent to your computers and AI services

When you connect, Tare communicates with the computer and bridge you configure. Messages, selected images, answers to questions and permission decisions are sent to that computer. Your configured coding agent may send messages, project context, images and tool output to its model provider or other configured services, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Cursor or Trae, depending on your setup. Those services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Only use accounts, machines and data you are authorized to access.

Permissions

Camera access lets you take a photo to attach to a message. Photo access lets you select images to attach. Local network access lets you reach computers you add on your network. These permissions are controlled in iOS Settings. Tare does not require microphone, location, contacts or advertising-tracking access.

Tracking and developer access

The app does not include advertising or analytics tracking. Tare does not automatically upload your conversation history to a developer-operated Tare cloud. Your computer, bridge administrator and configured agent services can process the data you send to them. If you email support, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your request.

Retention and deletion

Local caches and drafts remain in app storage until replaced or removed. Removing a device from Tare does not delete sessions or files on the remote computer. Deleting the app removes its app container; iOS may retain Keychain items separately. To delete remote conversations or provider-held data, use the controls on your computer or contact the relevant service. Do not send passwords, private keys, access tokens or private project contents to support.

Your choices and contact

You choose which computers to connect, what messages and images to send, and whether to grant requested permissions. Tare asks for AI data-sharing permission before sending to an agent. You can withdraw this permission in Settings > Reset AI permissions; this does not stop agents already running on your computers. You can disconnect machines and change iOS permissions. For privacy questions or assistance with data deletion, contact simpleyyt@gmail.com. Material changes to this policy will be reflected in its updated date.

中文说明

Tare 是连接用户自有电脑上编程智能体的独立客户端，不提供模型账号或订阅。连接设置、会话缓存、草稿、已读进度和偏好保存在手机本地；SSH 私钥和密码通过 iOS 钥匙串保存，其他连接信息（包括直连 bridge 凭据）保存在应用存储中。

你发送的消息、所选图片、问题回答和权限决定会传给你配置的电脑。电脑上的智能体可能向其配置的模型或工具服务传输消息、项目上下文、图片和工具输出，具体取决于你选择的服务及其隐私政策。Tare 不自动将会话上传到开发者运营的云端，也不包含广告或分析追踪。

相机与照片权限用于附加图片，本地网络权限用于连接你添加的电脑。应用不需要麦克风、定位、通讯录或广告追踪权限。你可以在 iOS 设置中管理权限。

删除设备不会删除远端会话与文件。删除应用会移除应用容器，但 iOS 可能单独保留钥匙串项目。远端会话及服务提供方保存的数据应通过相应电脑或服务管理。邮件支持仅使用你主动提供的信息协助处理问题，请勿发送密码、私钥、令牌或私人项目内容。

隐私问题或数据删除协助请联系 simpleyyt@gmail.com。
