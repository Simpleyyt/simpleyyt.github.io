Tare Support

Tare connects your iPhone to coding agents running on your own computers. You need a reachable computer, tare-bridge, and a supported agent with your own provider authentication. Tare does not include model access.

Getting started

  1. Install Node.js and run npm i -g tare-bridge on your computer.
  2. Configure and sign in to your chosen coding agent on that computer.
  3. Enable SSH access or configure a bridge endpoint reachable by your iPhone.
  4. Add your device in Tare, verify its SSH host fingerprint, and select a project and agent.

Use SSH or a trusted, protected connection. Do not expose an unauthenticated bridge to the public internet.

Connection or login problems

Check that the computer is awake, your network can reach it, and the agent is authenticated on that machine. Available models depend on the agent and provider account. Tare cannot grant access to an unsupported model.

Contact

Email simpleyyt@gmail.com with your app version, iOS version and a short description. Remove private information from screenshots and logs. Never send passwords, private keys or access tokens.
